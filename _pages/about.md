I am a Ph.D. student at Tianjin Medical University. My research interests include immunometabolism, metabolic–immune interactions, and AI for Science.

I am based in Tianjin, China, and my academic interests lie at the intersection of metabolism, immunology, and computational approaches to scientific discovery. This website brings together my academic background, publications, and conference activities.

## Research Interests

My research interests center on understanding how metabolic processes and immune responses influence one another. I am interested in connecting biological questions with approaches that can help us better understand these interactions.

- **Immunometabolism:** I am interested in how metabolism relates to immune-cell function, and how changes in metabolic state may shape immune responses.
- **Metabolic–immune interactions:** I am interested in the connections between metabolic regulation and immunity, including their relevance to metabolic disease.
- **AI for Science:** I am interested in exploring how artificial intelligence can support biological research, from organizing and interpreting scientific information to identifying questions for further investigation.

These interests guide the topics I follow in the literature and at academic meetings. I am keen to develop a perspective that connects biological mechanisms with computational thinking.
